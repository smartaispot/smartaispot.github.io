Top 5 Free AI Video Editing Tools in 2026

Published on September 2026 | Category: AI Tools & Media

Creating high-quality videos no longer requires hours of manual timeline editing or high-end technical skills. Modern AI-powered video tools allow creators to generate shorts, add automated captions, and apply complex effects within minutes.

1. CapCut AI – Auto-Subtitles & Script-to-Video

CapCut continues to dominate short-form video creation with powerful built-in text-to-speech, automated subtitles, and one-click video generation tools.

2. RunwayML Gen-2 – Text-to-Video Generation

Runway lets you turn text prompts into cinematic video clips, making it a game-changer for digital storytellers and content creators.

3. Descript – Edit Video Like a Text Document

Descript transcribes your uploaded audio and video. Simply delete or edit text in the generated document, and the tool instantly clips the video file for you.

4. InVideo AI – Instant Prompt to Full Video

Give InVideo a topic prompt, and it generates an entire YouTube Short or Reel—complete with script, stock footage, voiceover, and captions.

5. Clipchamp – Free Cloud-Based AI Editing

Microsoft's Clipchamp provides automatic video cropping, smart noise cancellation, and auto-generated captions right inside your browser.
